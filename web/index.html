About

Engineer by trade, artist by training.

I've built software professionally for ten years, most of it for Washington State's Employment Security Department, where I turn what people need into tools they use every day.

Since 2024 I've also designed and shipped the apps on this page on my own. I start by talking with the people who'll use the app, build something they can try quickly, and keep improving it from what they tell me.

Before code, I studied fine art and worked as an illustrator. That's why I care how things look and feel, not only whether they work.
